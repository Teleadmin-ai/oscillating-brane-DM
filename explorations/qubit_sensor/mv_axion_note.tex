\begin{abstract}
\noindent We present one concrete, falsifiable coordinate for the axion dark-matter program, derived
within an independent braneworld cosmology (Oscillating Brane Theory, OBT) whose type-IIB LVS
completion fixes the string scale $M_s = 1.19\times10^{12}\,\GeV$ and contains an ultra-light
K\"ahler-axion mode at the $\ueV$ scale ($\nu \simeq 242$~MHz central; order-of-magnitude bracket
$\sim 0.3\text{--}3~\ueV$). The photon coupling is $\gagg = (\alpha/2\pi)\,C/f_a$ with
$f_a \sim M_s$ natural, and the standard misalignment relic gives
$\Omega_a h^2 \simeq 1.2\times10^{-3} (f_a/M_s)^2\theta_i^2$ --- 1\% of the dark matter at the natural
point, 100\% at $f_a \simeq 1.2\times10^{13}\,\GeV$. Our main result is that this decay-constant
uncertainty does not propagate to the experiment: because the string-ALP mass is instanton-set
(independent of $f_a$, unlike the QCD axion), the density-weighted haloscope coupling is exactly
$f_a$-invariant below saturation,
$\geff \equiv g\sqrt{\Omega_a/\Omega_{\rm DM}} = (\alpha/2\pi)\,C\theta_i/f_{a,\rm DM}
= 9.8\times10^{-17}\,C\theta_i\;\GeV^{-1}$.
The flat floor crosses the QCD band inside the target decade and sits five orders of magnitude below
the CAST bound. The proposed FLASH haloscope (100--300~MHz) contains the central target: its declared
phase-1 sensitivity grazes the floor ($0.98\times$), and its phase-2 goal cuts a factor $\sim$5 below
it --- decisive for the entire bracket at $C,\theta_i = \mathcal{O}(1)$. We state the falsification
condition in advance: excluding the $\ueV$ decade at $\geff \lesssim 1\times10^{-17}\,\GeV^{-1}$ kills
the whole misalignment chain at $\mathcal{O}(1)$ angles.
\end{abstract}

spectrum contains the string scale $M_s = M_{\rm Pl}/\sqrt{\mathcal V} = 1.19\times10^{12}\,\GeV$
(volume $\mathcal V \approx 4\times10^{12}$) and an ultra-light modulus at
$m_V \sim 10^{-6}$~eV (order of magnitude). This $\ueV$ scale predates the axion chain; it was not
tuned toward any experiment.
\item \textbf{The identification} (\texttt{mv\_coupling.py}). The $\ueV$ mode is identified as the
imaginary (axion) partner of a blow-up/fibre K\"ahler modulus: the shift symmetry makes it
ultra-light with \emph{derivative couplings only} (no static fifth force, evading E\"otv\"os/Cassini

\begin{equation}
\gagg \;=\; \frac{\alpha}{2\pi}\,\frac{C}{f_a},
\qquad C = \mathcal{O}(1)\ \text{(unknown anomaly coefficient).}
\end{equation}
\item \textbf{The decay constant is not free} (\texttt{mv\_fa\_lvs.py}). The LVS axiverse

\item \textbf{The relic abundance} (\texttt{mv\_abundance.py}; standard misalignment

i.e.\ $\sim$1\% of the dark matter at the natural point, rising to 100\% at
$f_a \simeq 1.2\times10^{13}\,\GeV$ (the LVS high end; over-closure forbids larger $f_a$ at
$\theta_i\sim1$). In OBT the \emph{dominant} dark matter is geometric (a projected-Weyl braneworld

corner instead pushes toward low-scale inflation).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Main result: the $f_a$-invariant effective coupling}
\label{sec:invariance}
A haloscope measures the power $g^2\rho_a$, i.e.\ the effective coupling
$\geff = g\sqrt{\Omega_a/\Omega_{\rm DM}}$ (assuming the axion fraction clusters like the bulk dark
matter). In this chain $g \propto 1/f_a$ while $\Omega_a \propto f_a^2$ --- because the string-ALP
mass is instanton-set, \emph{independent of $f_a$} (unlike the QCD axion) --- so the $f_a$ dependence
cancels exactly below saturation:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;\geff \;=\; \frac{\alpha}{2\pi}\,\frac{C\,\theta_i}{f_{a,\rm DM}}
\;=\; 9.8\times10^{-17}\; C\,\theta_i\ \GeV^{-1},\qquad
f_{a,\rm DM} = 1.2\times10^{13}\,\GeV,\;}
\label{eq:inv}
\end{equation}
verified numerically to $10^{-9}$ across the three-decade LVS bracket
(\texttt{mv\_effective\_coupling.py}). \textbf{The apparent $f_a$ uncertainty (a 1\%--100\%
dark-matter fraction) does not propagate to the experiment: every point of the bracket presents the
same haloscope target.} The residual theory spread is the honest $\mathcal{O}(1)$ pair $C\theta_i$
and the order of magnitude on the $\ueV$ mass itself.

\section{The experimental match: FLASH and the $\mu$eV-decade program}
The proposed \textbf{FLASH} haloscope (FINUDA magnet, Frascati \cite{FLASH2023}) is declared for
100--300~MHz (QCD-axion masses 0.49--1.49~$\ueV$) --- the OBT central target (242~MHz) sits
\emph{inside} that band:
\begin{itemize}
\item FLASH \textbf{phase 1} (microstrip SQUID, DFSZ-class $\sim\!1\times10^{-16}\,\GeV^{-1}$): the
OBT floor is $0.98\times$ that depth --- phase 1 \emph{grazes the whole target}.
\item FLASH \textbf{phase 2} (100~mK, $\sim\!2\times10^{-17}\,\GeV^{-1}$): cuts a factor $\sim$5
below the floor --- decisive for the entire $f_a$ bracket at $C\theta_i = \mathcal{O}(1)$.
\item The wings of the mass bracket (0.3--0.5 and 1.5--3~$\ueV$, i.e.\ 73--120 and 360--725~MHz)
fall to the neighboring programs (lumped-element / DM-Radio-class below; the historic and
next-generation cavity program above \cite{ADMX2018,ADMX2021}, where reaching the floor means
DFSZ/2 to DFSZ/5 depth). Qubit single-photon counting \cite{Dixit2021} is the readout class that
accelerates such scans.
\end{itemize}

\section{Falsifiability (declared before any data)}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Exclusion:} a scan of the $\sim$0.3--3~$\ueV$ decade at
$\geff \lesssim 1\times10^{-17}\,\GeV^{-1}$ ($\approx$ floor/10) \textbf{kills the entire
misalignment chain at $\mathcal{O}(1)$ angles} --- the OBT axion bone dies cleanly (the theory's
core is separate; this falsifies its one derived direct-detection channel). FLASH phase 2 alone
already bites the central half-decade at floor/5.
\item \textbf{Detection:} a signal at $\ueV$ masses with $\geff$ within $\sim$$\times$3 of
$10^{-16}\,\GeV^{-1}$ matches the chain; the OBT-vs-QCD-axion discriminant is then the $(m, g)$
position relative to the QCD relation (the flat floor is mass-independent; the QCD band scales
$g \propto m$ --- distinguishable away from the 0.25--0.64~$\ueV$ crossings). A detection \emph{on}
the QCD relation would remain ambiguous --- we state this plainly.
\item \textbf{What we will not do:} claim support from a null that only grazes the floor, or dress an
anomaly elsewhere as this field. (Our own quantum-hardware protocol pre-registers the same
discipline.)
\end{itemize}

prediction) --- hence the decade-wide mass bracket.
\item $C$ and $\theta_i$ are $\mathcal{O}(1)$ unknowns (the floor scales linearly in each).
\item \textbf{Invariance assumptions:} $m_a$ independent of $f_a$ (string ALP); standard
misalignment with no post-$T_{\rm osc}$ entropy injection; below over-closure saturation; local
axion fraction proportional to the bulk fraction.
\item $f_a \sim M_s$ natural within a factor $\sim\mathcal{O}(10)$ ($2\pi$'s, volume suppressions)
--- \emph{absorbed} by the invariance for $\geff$, but relevant to the raw $g$ if a
density-independent probe is used.
\end{enumerate}
