\section{Advantages Over Competitors}
\label{sec:advantages}

\subsection{Advantages vs \LCDM}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Explains DM--baryon coupling naturally via geometric oscillation.
  \item No need for undiscovered particles---two decades of null results at LZ/XENONnT are a \emph{prediction}, not a puzzle.
  \item Resolves small-scale problems (cusp-core, missing satellites, RAR) without baryonic feedback fine-tuning.
  \item Provides unified framework: DM + DE from a single geometric mechanism (brane oscillation).
  \item Naturally produces scale-dependent $S_8$ suppression reconciling DES and KiDS.
  \item Explains DESI's evolving dark energy without ghost fields or phantom crossing.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Advantages vs MOND}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Works at \emph{all} scales (galaxies to cosmology)---MOND fails at cluster scales.
  \item No need for complicated relativistic extensions (TeVeS, AQUAL)---the framework is inherently relativistic (5D GR).
  \item Explains cluster dynamics and gravitational lensing (Bullet Cluster) naturally.
  \item Fully compatible with CMB observations without requiring $2\eV$ neutrinos.
  \item Consistent with gravitational wave speed $c_\text{GW} = c$ (GW170817).
  \item MOND's $a_0 = cH_0/2\pi$ emerges as a \emph{derived} quantity, not a postulate.
\end{itemize}

\section{Unique Signatures}
\label{sec:unique_signatures}

The following signatures are \emph{unique} to the oscillating brane model and cannot be reproduced by \LCDM{} or MOND:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Permanent null results in direct detection}: The model predicts $\sigma < 10^{-48}$~cm$^2$ \emph{forever}---no particle will ever be found because dark matter is geometric.

  \item \textbf{ISW signature in CMB}: The $2\Gyr$ oscillation creates a specific resonance pattern at $\ell = 10$--$20$ that no particle dark matter model can produce.

  \item \textbf{Scale-dependent growth suppression}: The Yukawa form $\Geff(k) = \GN(1 + \alpha\,e^{-k/\kL})$ produces a specific $k$-dependent signature distinguishable from uniform suppression.

  \item \textbf{21\,cm spatial modulation}: The oscillating $\Geff(k,t)$ imprints a $\sim 1$--$5$~mK modulation on the reionization 21\,cm signal---a smoking gun.

  \item \textbf{Baryon--DM coupling}: Tighter correlation than \LCDM{} expects, with predictable deviations in ultra-diffuse galaxies.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Falsification Criteria}
\label{sec:falsification}

The model would be \textbf{definitively falsified} by:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Direct detection of DM particles with $\sigma > 10^{-48}$~cm$^2$.
  \item Absence of ISW resonance signature in upcoming CMB-S4 surveys.
  \item Discovery of DM-dominated structures without baryons.
  \item Variations in fundamental constants beyond $|\dot{G}/G| > 10^{-13}$~yr$^{-1}$.
  \item SKA detection of a smooth 21\,cm power spectrum without the predicted $2\Gyr$ modulation.
  \item Euclid/DESI Year~5 confirmation that $w(z)$ is strictly constant ($w = -1$) at $> 5\sigma$.
\end{itemize}

The theory makes specific, quantitative predictions across multiple observational channels. The coming decade will either confirm a revolutionary new understanding of cosmic dynamics or definitively rule it out. This epistemic vulnerability is a strength, not a weakness---it is the hallmark of genuine scientific theory.
